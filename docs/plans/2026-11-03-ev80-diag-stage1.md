**Goal:** a signed, sideloadable app (`io.github.ev80diag`) that connects listen-only to an OBDLink LX (STN, Bluetooth) or a gs_usb USB-CAN adapter (OTG), records the complete CAN traffic of every charge attempt or charge, shows the Charging screen, and shares a session as one `.ev80` file. It is also fully testable by replaying `CAN-logs/` files.

**Architecture:** the transport source thread only produces `CanFrame`s into a `FrameBus` (one bounded channel per consumer, drops counted per consumer). The consumers are:
- the raw logger (multi-member gzip candump text with a 2-min pre-roll);
- the decoder (data-driven signal table → `VanSnapshot` StateFlow);
- the session detector (Room).

Compose screens only collect StateFlows.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin 2.2, Jetpack Compose/Material 3, Room 2.8 (KSP), coroutines/Flow, Android Bluetooth RFCOMM + USB Host API, `java.util.zip`, JUnit 4. No new third-party libraries; charts are drawn with Compose `Canvas`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-ev80-diag-app-design.md` (§4–§8, §11 decisions, §12 hardware). Mockups: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-ev80-diag-mockups/charging-v4.html`, `connect-v2.html`, `more.html`.

## Global Constraints

- **Never transmit on CAN:**
  - no OBD requests (`0100` etc.);
  - STN monitor only (`STMA`); `ATCSM1` (silent monitoring) sent and checked;
  - gs_usb started with `GS_CAN_MODE_LISTEN_ONLY` (flag `1`);
  - an adapter that is not STN (`STI` answers `?`) is refused with `R.string.adapter_not_stn`.
- applicationId `io.github.ev80diag`; debug keeps `applicationIdSuffix ".dev"`; app label "EV80-diag". The Kotlin namespace `io.github.ev80diag` is kept (no package rename).
- minSdk 24, target/compileSdk 36. No new dependencies besides `androidx.appcompat` (already in the version catalog) for per-app locales.
- **Strings:** all user-visible text goes in `res/values/strings.xml` (EN), with German in `res/values-de/strings.xml`. No hard-coded UI text.
- **Raw log line format** (laptop-tool compatible, must match `tools/elm_bench.py` and `tools/can_live.py` regexes): `(%.6f) can0 %03X#%s` (uppercase hex data, no spaces).
- **Marks file format:** `(%.6f) MARK <text>` (same as `can_live.py`).
- **Van state rules:**
  - READY = `0x580` B1 bit `0x04` set AND `0x301` DC link > 300 V AND not charging;
  - charging = `0x200` B0 ∈ {0x15 AC, 0x16 DC}; 0x1A = DC complete; 0x12 = BMS stop;
  - gear = `0x230` B0 (5 D, 6 N, 7 R, 8 P);
  - `0x270`/`0x55C` are not used for READY.
- **Session rules (§6 + §11):**
  - a charge attempt starts when the BMS set (`0x200`) appears while not READY;
  - it becomes CHARGE_AC/CHARGE_DC on 0x15/0x16;
  - it ends 60 s after leaving 0x15/0x16, or after the BMS has been silent for 120 s (outcome NO_CHARGE if 0x15/0x16 was never reached);
  - gaps < 120 s don't split; drive = READY → not READY (gaps < 120 s don't split);
  - pre-roll 120 s.
- **Default settings:**
  - record full CAN traffic = charging only;
  - recording mode = only while app open;
  - drive raw retention 90 days, charge raw kept;
  - insulation low 180 kΩ (amber) / 36 kΩ (red);
  - cold battery 3 °C (amber) / 0 °C while charging (red).
- **Charts:** one y-axis per chart; power and insulation are two charts on a shared time axis; insulation on a log₁₀ scale 10 kΩ–10 MΩ with shaded zones; status colours always paired with icon + text.
- Dark theme default; all screens follow rotation (no `screenOrientation` lock).

## Review Focus

1. **Van falls asleep / wakes mid-session** (BMS silent ≤ 4 s, `CAN ERROR` lines): no monitor restart, no session split, no reconnect storm. Pinned in Task 4 (`canError_doesNotRestart`) and Task 7 (`shortGap_doesNotSplitSession`).
2. **Phone app killed or crash mid-recording:** everything flushed so far stays readable. Pinned in Task 6 (`truncatedLastMember_earlierLinesReadable`).
3. **Plain/clone ELM adapter selected:** refused before `STMA`, nothing sent beyond AT queries. Pinned in Task 4 (`plainElm_isRefused_noMonitorStarted`).
4. **Charge attempt that never reaches 0x16** (Failure A at CableCheck/PreCharge): recorded with pre-roll and listed as "attempt, no charge". Pinned in Task 7 (`attemptWithoutCharge_isRecordedAsNoCharge`).
5. **Consumer slower than the bus** (DB stall): frames dropped only for that consumer and counted; the raw logger is unaffected. Pinned in Task 3 (`slowConsumer_dropsCounted_otherConsumerComplete`).
