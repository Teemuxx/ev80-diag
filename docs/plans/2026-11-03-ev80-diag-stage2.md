# EV80-diag Stage 2 (Live, Tiles, Battery, Details) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** add the driving screens to the stage-1 app: Live (cell health first), Tiles (eight user-picked values), Battery (104-cell heatmap and cell chart over a chosen period) and Details (every verified and probable signal), with the spec's bottom bar.

**Architecture:**
- The pipeline's decode consumer gains three derived feeds: drive metrics (distance, energy, consumption), a load-change clock, and 104-cell snapshots stored in Room at 1 per 2 s while charging or 1 per 10 s otherwise. It also keeps the last raw frame per signal ID.
- Screens stay thin. Each has a pure builder (`LiveUiBuilder`, `TileValues`, `CellStats`/`CellSeries`, `DetailRows`) that turns `RecorderState` plus DB rows into display values and is unit-tested. The Compose code only lays them out.
- Room goes to version 2 by AutoMigration: a new `cell_snapshot` table, plus `distanceKm`/`driveKwh` columns on `session`.

**Tech Stack:** as stage 1 (Kotlin 2.2, Compose/Material 3, Room 2.8 KSP, coroutines, JUnit 4). Charts and the heatmap are drawn with Compose `Canvas`. The date and time pickers are Material 3's own. The only new artifact is `androidx.room:room-testing`, used for the migration test (androidTest only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-ev80-diag-app-design.md`. Stage-2 sections: §5 navigation and top bar, §5.2 Live, §5.3 Tiles, §5.5 Battery, §5.6 Details, §7 (cell snapshots), §9 (range and consumption), §10 (thresholds), §11 (state-aware warnings). Mockups: `live-driving-v2.html`, `battery-v3.html`, `more.html`.

**Builds on:** branch `ev80-diag-stage1` (stage 1 done, last commit `6c9328c`). The stage-1 plan is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-ev80-diag-stage1.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Unchanged from stage 1:**
  - never transmit on CAN;
  - all user-visible text in `res/values/strings.xml` (EN) and `res/values-de/strings.xml` (DE); `StringsParityTest` stays green;
  - one y-axis per chart, legends for ≥ 2 series, status colours only with icon + text;
  - dark theme default; every screen works in portrait and landscape (no orientation lock);
  - Kotlin package stays `io.github.ev80diag`.
- **Bottom bar (§5):**
  - Live · Tiles · Battery · Charging · Details · ⋯;
  - in portrait it shows Live, Tiles, Battery, Charging and ⋯, with Details inside ⋯;
  - ⋯ holds Connect, Export & Debug and Settings (Experimental is stage 3);
  - tapping the van name in the top bar also opens Connect.
- **Start screen:** Connect while not connected. When the link becomes CONNECTED while Connect is shown, jump to Live, or to Charging if a charge is running (§5.1).
- **Top bar:** in READY the state chip reads "READY · D" (gear letter from `gear`: 5 D, 6 N, 7 R, 8 P).
- **Thresholds (§10/§11):**
  - spread: amber at 80 mV, red at 160 mV;
  - lowest cell: amber below 3.10 V, red below 3.00 V;
  - spread warning suppressed while charging at the top (existing rule) and for 60 s after a load change;
  - cell colour scale saturates at ±60 mV around the pack median.
- **Cell bar on Live:** from 2.90 V (empty) to 3.65 V (full).
- **Power sign (my call):**
  - Live and Tiles show power = pack V × pack A / 1000, positive while driving (discharge) and negative for regen or charging, with the word "regen" next to a negative value;
  - the mockup's "−16.5 kW at 48 A" is inconsistent and is not followed;
  - the Charging screen keeps showing positive charge power.
- **Range (app), §9:** range = SOC_app/100 × capacityAh × packV / 1000 ÷ (kWh/100 km) × 100. Capacity comes from `SocTracker.snapshot.value.capacityAh`. Consumption comes from the 20 km window, or else the all-time drive average from Room; no consumption means no range ("–").
- **Consumption (my call):**
  - "now" = sliding 2 km window;
  - "recent" (used for range) = sliding 20 km window, but only once ≥ 5 km are in it;
  - both null below 0.2 km;
  - odometer frames reading 0 or going backwards are ignored.
- **Stale values:** Live, Tiles and Details must never show old values as current. When the link isn't CONNECTED or the van is OFF, values are dimmed and a line reads "Van asleep, values from HH:mm" (time = `van.lastFrameMs`). This comes from owner feedback 2026-11-03: old battery temperatures looked live.

## Review Focus

1. **Old values shown as live:** a sleeping van or an ended replay must show dimmed values and the "values from HH:mm" line. Pinned in Task 4 (`vanOff_marksStale`, `disconnected_marksStale`).
2. **Odometer glitch:** a `0x380` frame with odometer 0 appears at wake (seen 2026-10-27 18:42), and must not add 57,000 km or produce absurd consumption. Pinned in Task 3 (`odoZeroAndBackwards_ignored`).
3. **No cell data in the chosen period** (new install, a period before the first recording, or right after a replay of an old log): the Battery screen shows "No cell data in this period" and offers the recorded sessions, with no crash and no empty chart. Pinned in Task 6 (`emptyPeriod_hasNoSeries`) and Task 7 (empty state in the screen).
4. **Stored tile layout from an older version** (fewer than 8 entries, or an unknown tile name after a rename): fall back to the default for the broken slots, never crash. Pinned in Task 5 (`corruptLayout_fallsBackPerSlot`).
5. **Consumption near zero or negative** (standing with the heater on, long downhill): range must be "–", not huge or negative. Pinned in Task 3 (`range_nullForImplausibleConsumption`).
